## Design Context (Paper OS - Horizon Atelier)

### Product Truth

Paper OS is a **general-purpose, local-first document IDE** in the product class
of Cursor and VS Code, adapted for serious structured documents. The stable
product is the Workspace Kernel: files, search, editors, previews, local tools,
commands, status, and reviewable AI edits. Professional Packs add domain
semantics.

LaTeX is first-class, but not every Workspace is LaTeX. Mathematical modeling is
the first professional Pack; contest stages, ledgers, scripts, figures, and
submission checks must appear only when that Pack is active. Never present
roadmap collaboration, marketplace, or provider infrastructure as shipped.

### Users

Primary users are research groups, engineers, technical writers, students, and
teams producing long-form work with mixed Resources: LaTeX, Markdown, code,
data, images, PDFs, and bibliographies. They need a predictable editor, local
tool output they can inspect, AI grounded in real Workspace context, and a clear
answer to what changed and where important claims came from.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, serious, alive. Think *Cursor-class document tooling* x *craft
publishing house* x *trustworthy local toolchain*. Not a toy chatbot, generic AI
landing page, or competition dashboard. The application should feel like a
publication-grade IDE used every day.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Horizon Atelier** uses deep charcoal, a warm amber horizon signal, editorial
type on public surfaces, and exacting tool typography in the application.

Brand mark: **Quill Circle**, a conic-gradient amber ring with a serif `Q`, used
for identity, loading, and restrained AI state. It is a signal, not repeated
decoration.

Public site: cinematic and editorial, one primary idea per viewport, with a
real IDE/product image as the first-viewport signal. Horizon Glow may anchor the
bottom third of hero and campaign bands. Authentication may use the established
brand-aside plus form composition.

Application: quiet, dense, and work-focused. Use stable regions for activity,
Resource tree/Pack views, editor or preview, Assistant, bottom panels, and
status. Horizon Glow is reduced to a subtle edge/status treatment; never wash
the editor canvas in decorative light. Do not place the primary editor inside a
marketing card.

The **Magnifier** is a signature Evidence-capable view: a focused document
location plus a trace drawer showing source, result, script, data, and Artifact
links. It is available when the active Pack exposes evidence semantics, not a
global requirement for every Workspace.

Pack-specific experiences are semantic layers, not new chrome. A Markdown Pack
may use a calmer note-oriented document theme; a LaTeX Pack may expose PDF,
bibliography, and formula affordances; the mathematical-modeling Pack may expose
evidence and stage views. None may recolor or restructure the entire IDE.

### Application Readability

- Editor and primary body text must meet at least WCAG AA contrast; do not use
  muted gray for source text or primary labels.
- Use approximately 13-14px UI text, 12-13px mono tree/status text, and
  comfortable editor line height. Compact does not mean faint.
- Reserve Playfair Display for public/editorial headings and intentional empty
  states. Use Inter for dense controls and JetBrains Mono for source, paths,
  diagnostics, logs, and status.
- Active tabs, focus rings, selections, diagnostics, disabled controls, and
  dirty state must be distinguishable without relying on amber alone.
- Keep fixed chrome dimensions and responsive min/max constraints so labels,
  tabs, counters, and tool states do not shift the layout.
- Use ellipsis plus tooltip for long paths; never let Workspace names or file
  names overlap actions.
- In compact panels, use small, tight headings. Hero-scale typography belongs
  only to true public-site heroes.

### Color

- **Base**: `#08080a`, `#0a0a0c`, `#0f0f12` - deep charcoal, never pure black.
- **Raised surfaces**: `#151518`, `#1b1b1f` - enough separation to read panel
  boundaries without floating-card styling.
- **Primary accent**: `#d4a341`, `#e2b564` - brand mark, primary command, focus,
  active state, and restrained Horizon Glow.
- **Vermilion**: `#c44747` - errors and destructive actions.
- **Signal jade**: `#4d9978` - success, ready state, verified evidence.
- **Cool ink**: `#7e9bb8` - links, code/math accents, informational state.
- **Primary text**: `#e8e3da` - warm off-white, never pure `#fff`.
- **Secondary text**: use a contrast-checked neutral near `#b8b1a7`; reserve
  darker grays for disabled or tertiary metadata.

The application must not become a one-note amber/brown surface. Charcoal,
off-white, cool ink, jade, and vermilion carry distinct information roles.

### Type

- **Display**: Playfair Display 400/italic/700 for public editorial headings.
  The established italic final hero line remains a campaign device, not an app
  panel convention.
- **Body/UI**: `@fontsource-variable/inter` for controls, labels, and prose.
- **Mono**: JetBrains Mono 400/500 for source, paths, logs, diagnostics, and
  status.
- **Math**: STIX Two Math, then Latin Modern Math or Cambria Math. Formula
  previews should visually agree with compiled output where possible.

### Motion

- Public/campaign surfaces may use restrained `horizonPulse` (14s), `quillSpin`
  (18s), CTA sheen, and slow reveal/parallax when a real product image remains
  inspectable.
- Application motion communicates state: panel resize, provider switch, build
  progress, diff reveal, and command completion. Avoid ambient movement behind
  source text.
- Use expo-out or smooth-quint timing. Never use bounce or elastic easing.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; collapse nonessential motion and
  keep state changes understandable without animation.

### Visual Motifs

- **Quill Circle**: identity, loading, AI-thinking state; never a repeated badge
  on every panel.
- **Horizon Glow**: public hero/campaign band and subtle application status edge.
- **Magnifier**: brass-rimmed Evidence focus treatment, only in evidence-aware
  contexts.
- **Evidence panes**: thin amber-bordered traces for claim -> source -> result ->
  Artifact relationships; use within evidence-capable Packs.
- **Resource type cues**: restrained extension/language icons or chips for any
  Resource type, not only `.tex`, `.bib`, `.sty`, and `.pdf`.

### Layout and Components

- Use one Workbench frame, not nested cards. Page sections and editor regions
  are unframed bands/panels separated by borders or splitters.
- Cards are reserved for repeated entities such as recent Workspaces, Pack
  choices, marketplace items, or modal choices; keep radius at 8px or less.
- Use Lucide icons already available in the project for familiar commands.
  Prefer icon buttons for save, undo/redo, split, close, search, and panel tools,
  with tooltips and accessible labels.
- Workspace and Pack selection use a compact chooser/list, not `prompt()`.
  Rows show name, Pack, path hint, and state without marketing copy.
- Editor/preview and bottom panels use explicit resizable constraints and stable
  minimum dimensions. No control, label, or overlay may cover adjacent content.
- Assistant output that proposes edits must expose Diff, Apply, Reject, pending,
  conflict, and failure states. The visual model follows EditTransaction, not a
  chat message pretending a write already happened.

### Tone and Voice

- Concise, professional, and concrete. No cheerleading or “快来用啊” copy.
- Use Chinese body copy with stable technical terms: Workspace, Resource,
  Document, Pack, Capability, Build Profile, Build Job, Artifact, Evidence Link,
  Edit Transaction, LaTeX, MCP, Tectonic, and `paperctl`.
- General UI says **Workspace**, never Contest. “Contest” appears only inside the
  mathematical-modeling Pack or legacy compatibility messaging.
- Describe current state honestly: “planned” and “requires Pack” are preferable
  to decorative controls that imply availability.
- Product descriptor: “A local-first IDE for serious documents.”
- Evidence campaign line: “写作的每一段，都有出处。”
- The former “LaTeX native. Team first. AI in every paragraph.” line may be used
  for LaTeX-focused campaign material, but not as proof that team features ship.

### Anti-Slop (Never Do)

- Purple-blue AI gradients (`#667eea`, `#7c3aed`, or similar dominant schemes).
- Inter as the primary public display face.
- Glassmorphism on every surface; glass is limited to nav, command palette,
  campaign console, and Magnifier.
- Pure black `#000`, pure white `#fff`, weak-contrast gray source text, or amber
  body text on charcoal.
- Bounce/elastic easing.
- Centered three-equal-card grids repeated across every section.
- Cards inside cards or floating-card page sections.
- Decorative sparkline charts or fake activity/status data.
- Generic Raleway, Poppins, or Quicksand display typography.
- “Web 3.0”, “blockchain”, “synergy”, or generic AI-transformation copy.
- Contest-first onboarding, hard-coded LaTeX chrome in a general Workspace, or
  Pack actions visible without their Capability.
