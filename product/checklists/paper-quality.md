# Paper Quality Checklist

Use this before finalizing a contest paper.

## Direct Answers

- [ ] Every contest subquestion has a direct-answer paragraph.
- [ ] Direct answers contain numbers, units, or clear decision outputs where applicable.
- [ ] The abstract previews the actual results, not only the methods.

## Logic

- [ ] Problem analysis states mathematical nature and modeling route for every subquestion.
- [ ] Method choices explain why they fit the task.
- [ ] Rejected alternatives are recorded when tradeoffs matter.
- [ ] Assumptions have reasons and validation paths.

## Evidence

- [ ] Important results appear in `state/result-ledger.csv`.
- [ ] Figures appear in `state/figure-ledger.csv`.
- [ ] Source-dependent claims appear in `state/source-ledger.csv`.
- [ ] Abstract and direct-answer claims appear in `state/claim-ledger.csv`.
- [ ] Values are consistent across abstract, body, tables, and captions.

## Writing

- [ ] No vague claims such as "good", "reasonable", or "effective" without quantitative support.
- [ ] Every formula is motivated and interpreted.
- [ ] Every figure is interpreted in nearby text.
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis explains stability and boundary conditions.
